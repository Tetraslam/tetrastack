# source

where parts come from.

### [digi-key](https://www.digikey.com)
electronic parts, when one is needed tomorrow.

### [lcsc](https://www.lcsc.com)
the parts catalog behind jlcpcb assembly. pick parts here first for any board that jlcpcb will assemble.

### [mcmaster-carr](https://www.mcmaster.com)
screws, bearings, shafts and other mechanical hardware, next day, with a cad model for every part.

### [adafruit](https://www.adafruit.com)
sensor and driver breakout boards, on the [stemma qt](https://learn.adafruit.com/introducing-adafruit-stemma-qt) plug-in connector, so sensors attach without soldering.
