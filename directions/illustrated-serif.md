# illustrated serif

serif typography, an illustration with a sense of place, considered light and dark. fits personal and editorial sites.

## sources
- [tetraslam.world](https://tetraslam.world): the illustrated terrace, with separate day and night artwork, and serif text independent of the illustration.
- [its source](https://github.com/tetraslam/tetraslamworld): how the artwork swaps per theme without a flash.

## on the web
- [Maggie Appleton's site](https://maggieappleton.com/): distinctive serif typography with explanatory illustrations and a coherent botanical vocabulary. illustrations explain the ideas, and a small set of motifs repeats across navigation and content.

## avoid
a stock hero illustration with a serif slapped on. the illustration is of a place, and it changes with the light.
