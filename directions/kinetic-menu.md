# kinetic menu

a palette of two or three colors at full contrast, angled shapes, cut-out lettering, and a transition with personality on every action, while staying readable. fits games, playful consumer apps, anything where navigating is the experience.

## sources
- [original Persona 5 interface screenshots, categorized by menus, dialogue, inventory, and HUD](https://gameuidatabase.com/gameData.php?id=72): compare the explosive silhouettes around navigation with the comparatively orderly information inside menus. Use skewed containers, oversized selection states, and hard black/white contrast while keeping reading order stable. (the site expressly restricts AI asset-generation and machine-learning uses; do not treat its screenshots as reusable generation assets.)
- [original Japanese Persona 5 Royal promotional website, with gameplay screenshots and custom graphical navigation](https://p5r.jp/): study cutout typography, offset black backing shapes, star-shaped accents, and screenshots mounted in irregular frames. Repeat a small family of angles rather than rotating every element independently.
- [official international Persona 5 Royal website](https://persona.atlus.com/p5r/): examine how the visual language accommodates longer localized text, character selectors, and purchase controls. Keep the red ground and black silhouettes dominant; reserve white for readable content and emphatic actions.

## avoid
a red-and-black dashboard with every card tilted, every word outlined, and continuous motion unrelated to selection.
