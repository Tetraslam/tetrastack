# frutiger aero / y2k

glossy bubbles, sky and water, aqua glass, optimistic tech. fits consumer apps that should feel bright and hopeful.

## sources
- [original Mac OS X 10.0.4 screenshots documenting early Aqua](https://guidebookgallery.org/screenshots/macosx100): study luminous rounded controls, fine pinstriped surfaces, translucent materials, and distinct pressed/selected states. Concentrate gloss on interactive objects rather than applying the same gradient everywhere.
- [functioning Aero-styled website and image collection covering period interfaces, advertising, wallpapers, and hardware](https://frutigeraeroarchive.org/): examine glossy navigation, pictorial icons, blue/green color relationships, and the gallery’s original UI examples. Notice how photographic nature imagery and manufactured surfaces coexist without eliminating readable content panels.
- [curated collection of original Y2K-era visual artifacts, with an expandable gallery](https://cari.institute/aesthetics/y2k-aesthetic): study metallic and translucent objects, rounded technological forms, synthetic color, and compact futuristic lettering. Use this to distinguish Y2K materiality from later Aero’s airy, nature-oriented presentation.

## on the web
- [Webamp, a working browser Winamp with playlists, equalizer, skins, and visualizations](https://webamp.org/): dense metallic panels, luminous readouts, tiny controls whose states stay distinct. borrow the coordinated panel system and the visualization area, not the original control sizes.
- [7.css, a Windows 7 component library with live demos](https://khang-nd.github.io/7.css/): translucent window frames, glossy controls, inset fields, and layered borders. material treatment distinguishes draggable frames, editable areas, and actionable controls.

## avoid
merging chrome Y2K lettering, Vista glass, vaporwave grids, and floating dolphins into one indiscriminate nostalgia theme.
