# game boy / 8-bit

a four-shade palette, a pixel grid, bitmap fonts, chunky sprites. fits games, toys, nostalgic or very constrained interfaces.

## sources
- [Nintendo’s original Game Boy Tetris listing, with gameplay screenshots and trailer](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Games/Game-Boy/TETRIS--275924.html): study the strongly bounded playfield, narrow statistics column, tile-based borders, and differentiation through patterns rather than many colors. Keep pixel sizes and alignment consistent.
- [Nintendo’s Pokémon Red page, with original-game screenshots](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Games/Game-Boy/Pokemon-Red-Version-266109.html): study boxed dialogue, small directional selection markers, sparse battle layouts, and compact status bars. Build hierarchy through placement and framing before increasing font size.
- [live specimen page for an 8-bit-style web component library](https://nostalgic-css.github.io/NES.css/): inspect stepped corners, hard-edged borders, pixel shadows, and actual form controls. Useful for translating raster-era grammar into web components; its colorful NES vocabulary is distinct from monochrome Game Boy.
- [ModRetro's Chromatic, a modern Game Boy Color-compatible handheld](https://modretro.com/collections/chromatic-consoles): the hardware itself is the reference: translucent and solid shells, a real LCD playfield framed by a bezel label and a segmented trim stripe. take the bezel framing and the bright, toy-like product color against deep studio backdrops.
- [PICO-8, Lexaloffle's fantasy console](https://www.lexaloffle.com/pico-8.php): a fixed 16-color palette, 128×128 screen, and a 4×6 font, with the editor, sprite sheet, and music tracker in the same tiny pixel UI. take a hard palette constraint and one pixel grid shared by tools and content; its [cartridge BBS](https://www.lexaloffle.com/bbs/?cat=7) shows thousands of games living inside it.

## on the web
- [PICO-8, product site and playable showcase](https://www.lexaloffle.com/pico-8.php): cartridge thumbnails, pixel lettering, and a playable console in the middle make the limits tangible. a restricted palette and fixed visual units, with real interaction carrying the nostalgia.
- [Playdate, Panic's crank handheld](https://play.date/): the yellow object stays central, the screen is enlarged to show real games, and specs are plainly labeled. a modern pixel-screen product presented with confidence, not nostalgia.

## avoid
a pixel font on rounded modern cards, mixed pixel scales, and CRT scanlines imposed on an LCD-derived style.
