# lens

soft light, bloom, depth of field, focus pulls as transitions, pastel color, attention to tiny states. fits reflective, personal, and media products.

## sources
- [official Violet Evergarden episode gallery, with scene images and production-staff comments](https://tv.violet-evergarden.jp/story/): study sharp subjects against softer foreground and background layers, bright windows, and restrained warm/cool separation. Translate that into one clear focal plane, not blur applied uniformly across the interface.
- [official Liz and the Blue Bird production-video collection; volume 13 specifically covers photography/compositing](https://liz-bluebird.com/news/?id=36): use the photography installment to study how finished shots combine drawn layers and optical treatment. Adapt focus changes as transitions between foreground and background attention, keeping active text sharp.
- [official film website, including trailers, artwork, and a lightweight particle treatment](https://liz-bluebird.com/): study pale grounds, fine typography, generous spacing, and small drifting elements. Let atmospheric light sit behind a quiet reading surface rather than turning every control into translucent glass.

## on the web
- [Blindsight, an interactive production archive for a science-fiction short film](https://blindsight.space/memories/): images on a receding spiral, so navigating feels like moving a camera through memories. borrow the foreground/background separation and motion-dependent blur. this is the darker, spatial branch, not kyoani's warmth.

## avoid
pastel glassmorphism with random bokeh circles covering text and perpetual lens flare.
