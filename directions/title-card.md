# title card

black and white, a heavy mincho set huge, stretched or squeezed, in broken stacked layouts, cut in hard. fits launches, story-driven pages, and moments that should feel like an event.

## sources
- [archived original “EPISODE:1 / ANGEL ATTACK” title card](https://peoplesgdarchive.org/item/17077/neon-genesis-evangelion-title-card): build hierarchy through extreme differences in type size, condensed letterforms, and tightly packed lines. Treat the black field as composition, not unused space.
- [high-resolution original English title card for episode 18](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NGE_title_card_ep18_en.png): study the relationship between the small episode identifier and the oversized title. Preserve deliberate line breaks and asymmetrical placement instead of centering a conventional hero headline.
- [working browser-based Evangelion title-card generator](https://kychou.net/eva-title): experiment with separate controls for title lines, episode label, and alignment. Useful for testing how text length changes the composition; this is a contemporary interpretation, not an original production asset.
- [Fonts In Use: Neon Genesis Evangelion](https://fontsinuse.com/uses/28760/neon-genesis-evangelion): the japanese subtitle cards for all 26 episodes (the L-shaped 第壱話 使/徒、襲来, the inverted episode 15 嘘と沈黙), matisse EB mechanically compressed, helvetica on the english cards. the best single source. kana and punctuation set smaller than kanji in the same line; break the title somewhere unexpected.
- [Fontworks on Matisse EB in Evangelion](https://www.fontworks.co.jp/column/394/): the foundry on the L-shaped episode 1 subtitle and on 長体率, the compression ratio, which differs between the TV series and Rebuild.
- [EvaGeeks frame-by-frame next-episode previews](https://wiki.evageeks.org/FGC:Episode_01_NextEp_Previews) (also episodes 02, 07): the 次回予告 structure, a preview card, then footage under flat third-person narration, then "next time: title", the fan-service promise, and the title card. the only accent is inversion, used once.

## on the web
- jikai (private, `~/Programming/jikai`): weekly next-episode previews built from these sources.

otherwise none at the bar yet. kychou.net/eva-title is a counter-example: a yellow glow and a centered stack.

## avoid
generic white serif text centered on black, with red Japanese characters added as decoration.
