# braun functionalism

calm off-white and gray, one signal color, precise controls, nothing extra. fits utilities and devices.

## sources
- [Rams’s ten principles, published by Vitsœ and illustrated with original Braun and Vitsœ products](https://www.vitsoe.com/us/about/good-design): Make controls and information explain themselves. Use restrained navigation, plainly labeled content, and generous separation; remove decoration that competes with the user’s task.
- [Braun T3 pocket radio, 1958, by Dieter Rams and Hochschule für Gestaltung Ulm](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/4134): Organize the surface into a repeated field and one dominant control. Use consistent spacing, quiet neutral materials, and clear differences between passive information and manipulation.
- [Braun ET55 calculator, 1980, by Dieter Rams and Dietrich Lubs](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/3697): Group controls by function within a regular matrix. Use selective color and tonal contrast to distinguish special actions; keep labels short and their placement consistent.

## on the web
- [Teenage Engineering, a hardware identity carried onto the web: custom pictograms, compact labeling, precise product imagery, purposeful accent colors](https://teenage.engineering/): make the interface feel designed by the people who designed the object, carrying its labeling, proportions, and visual logic across both.
- [Analogue, carefully art-directed hardware presentation with restrained navigation, controlled typography, and exceptional product staging](https://www.analogue.co/): give the product visual authority. a quiet interface still has character through proportion, image composition, and type.

## avoid
Beige neumorphic cards, ornamental speaker grilles, and an orange button added without a functional reason.
