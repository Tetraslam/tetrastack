# warm paper

parc's look: warm off-white surfaces a few percent apart, ink-overlay states, one accent with a meaning. fits places people live in daily: social, messaging, notes.

## sources
- parc's tokens (`src/styles/tokens.css` in the private parc repo, worked example in [taste.md](../taste.md#constants)): surfaces stepped side < bg < s1 < s2, hover and press as translucent ink at fixed strength so they nest, ember as the only hue.
- [the composer and field in "the bar"](../taste.md#the-bar): the level of care, not the look.

## avoid
copying parc's exact hex values and ember. derive a new paper tone and a new accent with its own meaning.
