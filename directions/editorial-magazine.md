# editorial / magazine

a strong grid, big headlines, pull quotes, columns, image and type in conversation. fits writing, publications, long reads.

## sources
- [The Paris Review’s publication site, integrating stories, quarterly issues, interviews, and archival material](https://www.theparisreview.org/): Distinguish content types through recurring labels and hierarchy. Keep bylines, excerpts, and issue information available without letting metadata overpower titles.
- [It’s Nice That’s visual-culture publication](https://www.itsnicethat.com/): Use image-led modules, varied story prominence, and clear category navigation to support browsing. Let commissioned imagery provide color instead of giving every article an artificial colored card.
- [Emigre’s archive of its 69 magazine issues](https://www.emigre.com/Magazine): Study purposeful departures from the grid, expressive type scale, and issue-to-issue variation. Experimental spreads work because relationships between text and image are composed, not randomly disrupted.

## avoid
A giant masthead above an endless uniform card grid, or decorative pull quotes that interrupt reading without adding meaning.
