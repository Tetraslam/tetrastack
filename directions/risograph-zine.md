# risograph / zine

two or three spot inks, misregistration, grain, photocopied texture, hand-made layouts. fits indie publishing, communities, small-batch creative work.

## sources
- [Emily Carr University’s scanned Risograph ink-density and two-ink overprint specimens](https://palette.ecuad.ca/comdtech/risograph/colour-reference-charts/): Choose two ink families and derive additional colors through their overlap and coverage levels. Texture should vary with ink density; avoid adding uniform noise over the entire interface.
- [RISOTTO’s own print-studio website and shop, showing its stationery, posters, and print projects](https://risottostudio.com/): Carry the printed palette into large UI fields and concise display typography. Let detailed print texture live mainly in actual artwork, preserving crisp navigation and readable product information.
- [Cornell’s archival display of original *Punk*, *Sniffin’ Glue*, and other fanzines, with image galleries](https://exhibits.library.cornell.edu/punkfest/feature/rise-of-the-fanzines): Pair urgent handwritten display text with economical typed body copy and abrupt photographic crops. Borrow the contrast between editorial voices, not arbitrary distress. This is the photocopied-zine lineage, not Risograph printing.

## avoid
Pink-and-blue gradients with a grain overlay, or distressed body text that sacrifices reading for “DIY authenticity.”
