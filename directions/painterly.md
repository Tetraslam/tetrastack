# painterly

paint and watercolor surfaces, often as an atmospheric backdrop behind a precise technical artifact, as on prime intellect's inference page. fits research, creative tools, and anything that should feel made by hand.

## sources
- [Maxime Heckel’s original research and interactive WebGL demonstrations of Kuwahara-based painterly rendering](https://blog.maximeheckel.com/posts/on-crafting-painterly-shaders/): Smooth interior detail while preserving important edges; orient filtering along local structure. Tune brush scale, color quantization, and paper texture together rather than applying a blur over the scene.
- [A production literary website using painterly imagery and ink-like image reveals](https://www.davidwhyte.com/): Keep typography and navigation crisp while imagery carries the organic motion. Let atmospheric transitions support reading rather than continuously animate behind text.
- [Niccolò Fanton’s runnable WebGL reconstruction of the David Whyte ink reveal, with source links and image input](https://ink.niccolofanton.dev/): Grow an irregular reveal front through layered noise and an animated threshold. Bias the reveal with depth so nearer forms emerge first instead of dissolving the entire image uniformly.

## on the web
- [the David Whyte Experience, an immersive poetry site](https://davidwhyte.com/experience/): serif type over ink-and-watercolor landscapes that bleed in as you go. painterly transitions pace the reading, and text keeps quiet areas of its own.

## avoid
A global paper-grain overlay, muddy blur, and generic opacity fades described as “watercolor.”
