# editorial grandeur

seriousness, restraint, hope. fits long-form and ideas.

## sources
- [Palladium’s magazine homepage, with essay headlines, artwork, excerpts, and author/date metadata](https://www.palladiummag.com/): Give titles and images enough space to establish intellectual weight. Keep supporting metadata visibly subordinate, and use long-form editorial hierarchy rather than sales-page headings.
- [Stripe Press’s catalog and book presentations](https://press.stripe.com/): Treat covers as substantial designed objects. Give each title its own color identity while maintaining disciplined typography and generous separation between books, descriptions, and endorsements.
- [St Bride Library’s digitized copy of Bodoni’s *Manuale tipografico*](https://archive.org/details/20771_202210): Study high-contrast letterforms, centered specimen compositions, and the amount of unprinted space surrounding type. Reserve delicate display faces for sufficiently large sizes.

## avoid
Beige backgrounds, oversized italic serif slogans, and arbitrary “EST. 1894” details masquerading as cultural authority.
