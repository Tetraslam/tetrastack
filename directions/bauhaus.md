# bauhaus

primary colors, circles, squares, and triangles, type as construction. fits education, tools, anything about form and function.

## sources
- [Joost Schmidt’s poster for the 1923 Bauhaus exhibition](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/6235): Construct a composition from interlocking circles, bars, and a shared diagonal. Let lettering participate in that geometry while keeping functional interface copy horizontal.
- [Herbert Bayer’s 1923 Bauhaus exhibition postcard](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/189328): Study how compact typography and geometric forms occupy the same small field. Use changes of direction and weight selectively, with clear boundaries between display lettering and supporting information.
- [Bayer’s wall-painting study for the Weimar Bauhaus stairwell, 1923](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/178202): Use colored planes to articulate actual spatial divisions. In a UI, assign color to navigation zones, section boundaries, or functional groups rather than floating decorative shapes.
