# bento grid

content in tidy tiles of different sizes, each one a self-contained idea. fits overviews, dashboards, product pages with many facets.

## sources
- [An archived copy of Bento’s own team page, built with its mixed-size content widgets](https://web.archive.org/web/20240228122720/https://bento.me/aboutus): Mix portraits, social links, and editorial blocks using a small family of tile sizes. Keep gutters and corner treatment consistent while allowing content to determine each tile’s composition.
- [Apple’s product presentation, combining large product imagery with compact feature and specification modules](https://www.apple.com/mac-mini/): Let major product claims occupy larger areas and subordinate specifications occupy smaller ones. Alternate photography, short text, and numerical emphasis without giving every module equal visual weight.
- [Microsoft’s illustrated explanation of the Windows 8 Start screen, 2012](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/askperf/windows-8--windows-server-2012-start-screen-and-user-interface-part-1/375111): Study modular square and double-width tiles, explicit groups, and information visible before opening an application.

## on the web
none at the bar yet. the strongest current examples (Raycast, Linear) are the generic-dark-devtool attractor. Apple's product pages, in the sources above, remain the reference.

## avoid
Arbitrary rounded rectangles containing identical icon-heading-paragraph stacks, with empty oversized tiles added only to complete a mosaic.
