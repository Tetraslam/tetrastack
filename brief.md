# design brief

write this as `BRIEF.md` in the new project before any ui code. keep it to a page. if the user is around, show it and wait for a yes. if not, commit it and build from it. either way it's the contract for the look, so update it when the look changes.

```md
# brief

## the product
what it is, who uses it, and the one thing they come to do.

## the feeling
two or three words for how it should feel, and one sentence on why that fits this product.

## direction
the direction from tetrastack's taste.md (or a deliberate blend, or a new one) and why it fits.
references picked for this product (2 or 3), and the principle taken from each. no signature moves.

## identity
- palette: surfaces, ink, accent, and what the accent means here
- type: reading and display faces, if any, beyond inter and commit mono
- shape: radius, borders, density
- texture and shaders: what lives where, and on which surfaces
- motion: named curves and durations, and the character of movement

## hierarchy
for the main screens: what the reader needs first, second, and what recedes.

## not parc
the closest of my existing projects in look, and three concrete ways this one differs from it.
```
