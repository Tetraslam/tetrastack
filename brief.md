# design brief

write this as `BRIEF.md` in the new project before any ui code. keep it to a page. the agent decides on its own: it weighs the candidates, picks, commits the brief, and builds from it without waiting for approval. the user reviews the result and can veto it later. it's the contract for the look, so update it when the look changes.

```md
# brief

## the product
what it is, who uses it, and the one thing they come to do.

## the feeling
two or three words for how it should feel, and one sentence on why that fits this product.

## candidates
three directions from tetrastack's taste.md that could fit, each with a one-line pitch for this product. at least one must be unexpected for this kind of product. none may be an attractor unless argued for.

## direction
the pick (or a deliberate blend, or a new one), why it beats the other two, and why it isn't an attractor.
references picked for this product (2 or 3), and the principle taken from each. no signature moves.

## identity
- palette: surfaces, ink, accent, and what the accent means here
- type: reading and display faces, if any, beyond inter and commit mono
- shape: radius, borders, density
- texture and shaders: what lives where, and on which surfaces
- motion: named curves and durations, and the character of movement

## hierarchy
for the main screens: what the reader needs first, second, and what recedes.

## not parc
the closest of my existing projects in look, and three concrete ways this one differs from it.
```
